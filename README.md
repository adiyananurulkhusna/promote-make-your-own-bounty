A make.fun bounty-board poster for the "Promote make.fun's make-your-own-bounty post on X" bounty. This bounty is scored on replies + reposts + quotes — likes and views count for nothing — so the post is a question, not a punchline: "You have $250 in USDC. What do you pay an AI agent to build?" Three deliberately silly seed ideas (a bot that answers my group chat, an app that finds my lost socks, a game where my cat is the final boss) give every reader something to beat, and the call to action is explicit: reply with your bounty idea, best idea gets a repost.

The poster is built with pure PIL (`build_poster.py`), in the same house style as the v6 verdict poster: cream paper, navy frame, gold rule, magenta accents, and make.fun's real ghost mascot (from the press kit at https://make.fun/press/) inside a magenta seal disc. It stays readable when shrunk to 480 by 480 pixels.

The entry is posted on X from the public account `@Adiyamanmonad` as a quote of make.fun's make-your-own-bounty post, with the poster as its photo, a tag of `@makefunai`, the sponsor link, and the account's play code in the post's text. The post's address goes in `POST.json`.

## Run it

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_BOT_DIR=. MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts
```
